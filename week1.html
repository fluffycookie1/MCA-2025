Week 1 Task: Creating my own theme for my portfolio!

For this weeks task, I have chosen my theme for my portfolio. My theme will be Latin and Hispanic music. My reason for choosing this as my theme is because Latin music has a very unique and original sound. Artists which fall under the umbrella term of Latin music are extremely talented, and I will be using two artists music throughout the tasks in my portfolio. These artists are La Rosalia and The Marias.

Challenges of working with music-related data:

Some challenges of working with music and music-related data can include aspects like limited access. Music is not always readily available for everyone, music streaming platforms can require subscription payments, and sometimes this cannot make all music available. Another issue can be copyright, music is a heavily monetised art, which means that if an individual wants to use a piece of music within their work, they would either need permission from the artists/music companies and would most likely need to pay for using the music. The data behind my chosen theme is presently curated mostly through online streaming platforms, and it is presented within algorithms to the individuals who would most likely be interested.
