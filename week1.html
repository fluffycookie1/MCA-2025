For this weeks task, I have chosen my theme for my portfolio to be 'The Marias'. They are a band from Los Angeles, however they write and preform their songs in both English and Spanish. They use a wide range of instruments, and the lead singer, Maria Zardoya, has a unique voice. When all these elements come together, it gives the band a very unique sound and makes them one of a kind.
