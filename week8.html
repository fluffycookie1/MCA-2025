Task 2: Describe at least one advantage of a time-frequency analysis over a waveform-based analysis.

A time frequency analysis has an advantage over a waveform analysis because a time frequency spectrogram shows us the signals of what frequencies are present and how they change throughout the song, this means it can benefit more in terms of analysing a variety of different sounds and frequencies such as voices, speech, animal sounds. It also has clearer indications of how the timbre and pitch change throughout the audio file, whereas the waveform shows when a sound happens and the amplitude of it. Another advantage of a time frequency analysis is that there can be clearer visual representations of when and how loud musical notes are when they play, whereas with a raw waveform analysis, this would be a bit more unclear.
